About the company

A reliable bridge between
Korea and Kazakhstan

Since 2015, KOREANA has been the official importer and distributor the best Korean products in the Republic of Kazakhstan.

We present a wide range of high quality brands for hair care, oral care mouth, household chemicals, feminine hygiene and cosmetics from leading South Korean manufacturers: AEKYUNG, MUKUNGHWA, JOONGWON, MYUNGIN COSMETICS.

Since 2025, the range has been expanded to include a line of sulfate-free Hair shampoos Clinic, men's line PRO ACTIVE MAN.

We don't just supply products - we develop brands in the market Kazakhstan, providing partners with marketing support, stable supplies and commercial effective model of cooperation.

The company's product range is constantly expanding - we regularly introduce new products and adapt our portfolio according to current market trends.

Message from the General Director

When we created KOREANA, our goal was not just to build trading distribution company.

We wanted to create an organization that would introduce consumers in Kazakhstan and Central Asia with quality international brands, creating new standards in categories beauty, personal care, hygiene and household chemicals, as well as being a reliable partner for producers, customers and employees.

Our journey began with the belief that long-term success is built on trust, reputation and daily work on quality. Over the years, we have built partnerships with leading production companies from South Korea, formed a professional team and created a stable platform for further business growth.

But we consider our main achievement not the number of brands or sales volumes, but trust people who work with us, buy our products and choose us as a partner.

We believe that business should create value for society. Therefore, every day is ours the team is working to make quality products more accessible to consumers, modern international brands have developed in local markets, and company employees received opportunities for professional and personal growth.

We continue to develop the company, investing in people, processes and technologies to create a sustainable business that our employees, partners and clients will be proud of.

Thank you to everyone who is part of this journey.

Support for Almaty Marathon, Astana Marathon and many others

We are a regular sponsor and partner of the country's largest running events - Almaty Marathon, Astana Marathon, Tau Jarys Marathon, Summer Relay Marathon and many others.

By supporting these initiatives, we contribute to the development of mass sports and promotion healthy lifestyle among Kazakhstanis. Every year thousands of participants take to the start line, and we We are proud to be part of this large-scale movement.

Our brands help athletes and enthusiasts stay clean, fresh and take care of their comfort after the finish. Our KOREANA team also participates in races every year, proving its commitment to an active lifestyle by example.

Thanks to all participants and organizers for their incredible energy and desire to win!

The best partner of AEKYUNG

Based on the results of 2023, KOREANA LLP was recognized as the best partner of AEKYUNG Co.

We are proud that our long-term cooperation with the largest South Korean manufacturer of household appliances chemistry and cosmetics brought such a high reward. Our team ensures stable supplies, wide market presence and high level of trust among consumers.

This award is the result of the coordinated work of our entire team and proof that we choose only the best for our clients!

Association of Koreans of Kazakhstan and charity

KOREANA LLP is a permanent participant in social and charitable projects, supporting all initiatives of the Association of Koreans of Kazakhstan.

Our company deeply appreciates its historical roots and actively participates in the life of the Korean diaspora in Kazakhstan. We sponsor cultural events, national holidays and educational initiatives aimed at preserving traditions and language.

In addition to cultural projects, KOREANA regularly provides charitable assistance to the needy, children's homes and medical institutions. We are convinced that business should be socially responsible and benefit the society in which it develops.

Support for the kpop-stars.kz festival

Our company KOREANA and the Kerasys brand are official partners of one of the most large-scale K-POP events - festival kpop-stars.kz.

Every year we support this exciting event. Over two days, the festival attracts a huge number of guests, participants and fans of Korean culture. The atmosphere, organization and audience involvement are always remain at the highest level.

For us, this is not just participation in the event - it is working on brand recognition, strengthening emotional connection with a young audience and development of the image of Korean brands in Kazakhstan.

Industry Asset Award

KOREANA LLP based on the results of 2022/2023/2025 (OKED 46.90.9) received a national "Industry Asset" award.

This status is awarded to companies that make a significant contribution to economic development Kazakhstan, demonstrate high reliability and sustainable growth. National recognition confirms our leading position in the non-specialized wholesale trade industry.

We are proud of this award as it reflects the quality of our work, the professionalism of our team and trust of our partners. This achievement motivates us to continue to develop and expand our range and provide the best service in the market of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

Support for student activity and youth

KOREANA LLP is actively investing in the future, acting as a regular partner of youth and student events.

We support open days at leading universities, sponsor student initiatives and festivals. It is important for us to be close to the younger generation, help them realize their potential and create opportunities for development and networking.

Our company sees students and young people as future leaders, professionals and innovators. Sponsoring such events, we not only increase awareness of our brands among young audiences, but also We make our contribution to the development of the educational and cultural environment of Kazakhstan.

Certification and quality standards
