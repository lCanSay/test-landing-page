About the Company

A Reliable Bridge Between
Korea and Kazakhstan

Since 2015, KOREANA has been the official importer and distributor of the finest Korean products in the Republic of Kazakhstan.

We present a wide range of high-quality brands for hair care, oral care, household chemicals, feminine hygiene and cosmetics from leading South Korean manufacturers: AEKYUNG, MUKUNGHWA, JOONGWON, MYUNGIN COSMETICS.

Since 2025, our range has expanded with the Hair Clinic sulfate-free shampoo line and the PRO ACTIVE MAN men's line.

We don't just supply products – we develop brands in the Kazakhstan market by providing our partners with marketing support, stable supplies, and a commercially effective cooperation model.

Our product range is constantly expanding – we regularly launch new products and adapt our portfolio to current market trends.

Support for Almaty Marathon, Astana Marathon and many others

We are a regular sponsor and partner of the country's largest running events - Almaty Marathon, Astana Marathon, Tau Jarys Marathon, Summer Relay Marathon and many others.

By supporting these initiatives, we contribute to the development of mass sports and the promotion of a healthy lifestyle among Kazakhstanis. Every year thousands of participants take to the start line, and we are proud to be part of this massive movement.

Our brands help athletes and amateurs maintain cleanliness and freshness, and take care of their comfort after the finish line. Our team KOREANA also participates in races every year, proving its commitment to an active lifestyle by its own example.

Thanks to all participants and organizers for the incredible energy and drive to win!

Best partner of AEKYUNG company

LLP "KOREANA" based on the results of 2023 year recognized as the best partner of AEKYUNG company Co.

We are proud that many years of cooperation with the largest South Korean manufacturer of household chemicals and cosmetics brought such a high award. Our team provides stable supplies, wide market presence and a high level of consumer trust.

This award is the result of the coordinated work of our entire team and proof that we choose only the best for our clients!

Association of Koreans in Kazakhstan and charity work

LLP "KOREANA" is a regular participant in social and charitable projects, supporting all initiatives of the Association of Koreans in Kazakhstan.

Our company deeply values historical roots and actively participates in the life of the Korean diaspora in Kazakhstan. We sponsor cultural events, national holidays and educational initiatives aimed at preserving traditions and language.

In addition to cultural projects, KOREANA regularly provides charitable assistance to the needy, orphanages and medical institutions. We are convinced that business should be socially responsible and benefit the society in which it operates.

Support for kpop-stars.kz festival

Our company KOREANA and brand Kerasys are official partners of one of the most massive K-POP events - the festival kpop-stars.kz.

We support this vibrant event annually. Over two days, the festival gathers a huge number of guests, participants, and fans of Korean culture. The atmosphere, organization, and audience engagement are always at the highest level.

For us, it's not just about participating in an event – it's about building brand awareness, strengthening emotional connection with the young audience, and developing the image of Korean brands in Kazakhstan.

Industry Heritage Award

LLP "KOREANA" based on the results of 2022 / 2023 / 2025 years (NACE 46.90.9) received the national award "Industry Heritage".

This status is awarded to companies that make a significant contribution to the development of the economy of Kazakhstan, demonstrating high reliability and sustainable growth. National recognition confirms our leading position in the non-specialized wholesale trade industry.

We are proud of this award as it reflects the quality of our work, the professionalism of our team, and the trust of our partners. This achievement motivates us to continue growing, expanding our assortment, and providing the best service in the Republic of Kazakhstan.

Support for student activities and youth

LLP "KOREANA" actively invests in the future, acting as a regular partner of youth and student events.

We support open days at leading universities, and sponsor student initiatives and festivals. It is important for us to be close to the younger generation, to help them realize their potential, and create opportunities for growth and networking.

Our company sees students and youth as future leaders, professionals, and innovators. By sponsoring such events, we not only increase brand awareness among young audiences, but also contribute to the development of the educational and cultural environment of Kazakhstan.

Certification and Quality Standards

All products presented by the company KOREANA, is officially registered and certified in the Republic of Kazakhstan.

We work exclusively with manufacturers who meet international quality standards, which guarantees product safety, efficiency, and consistency.

KOREANA is the official and exclusive representative of a number of Korean brands in Kazakhstan, which confirms the authenticity of products and direct supplies from the manufacturer. We strictly monitor compliance with all standards and requirements at every stage of the supply chain.
