About the Company

A Reliable Bridge Between
Korea and Kazakhstan

Since 2015, KOREANA has been the official importer and distributor of top Korean products across the Republic of Kazakhstan.

We present a wide range of premium brands for hair care, oral care, household chemistry, feminine hygiene, and cosmetics from South Korea's leading manufacturers: AEKYUNG, MUKUNGHWA, JOONGWON, and MYUNGIN COSMETICS.

In 2025, our assortment expanded to include the Hair Clinic sulfate-free shampoo line and the PRO ACTIVE MAN men's line.

We don't just supply products — we develop brands in the Kazakhstani market, providing our partners with marketing support, stable supplies, and a commercially efficient cooperation model.

Our product range is constantly expanding. We regularly introduce new items and adapt our portfolio to current market trends.

Supporting the Almaty Marathon, Astana Marathon, and more

We are a permanent sponsor and partner of the country's largest running events — Almaty Marathon, Astana Marathon, Tau Jarys Marathon, Summer Relay Marathon, and many others.

By supporting these initiatives, we contribute to the development of mass sports and the promotion of a healthy lifestyle among Kazakhstanis. Every year, thousands of participants take the starting line, and we are proud to be part of this massive movement.

Our brands help athletes and enthusiasts maintain cleanliness and freshness, ensuring their comfort after the finish line. The KOREANA team also participates in the races annually, demonstrating our commitment to an active lifestyle by our own example.

Thank you to all participants and organizers for the incredible energy and drive to win!

Best Partner of AEKYUNG

Based on the results of 2023, KOREANA LLP was recognized as the Best Partner by AEKYUNG Co.

We are proud that our long-term cooperation with the largest South Korean manufacturer of household chemistry and cosmetics has yielded such a high award. Our team ensures stable supplies, broad market representation, and a high level of consumer trust.

This award is the result of our team's coordinated work and proof that we choose only the best for our clients!

Association of Koreans of Kazakhstan and Charity

KOREANA LLP is a regular participant in social and charitable projects, supporting all initiatives of the Association of Koreans of Kazakhstan.

Our company deeply values its historical roots and actively participates in the life of the Korean diaspora in Kazakhstan. We sponsor cultural events, national holidays, and educational initiatives aimed at preserving traditions and language.

In addition to cultural projects, KOREANA regularly provides charitable assistance to people in need, orphanages, and medical institutions. We believe that business should be socially responsible and benefit the community in which it operates.

Sponsorship of the kpop-stars.kz festival

Our company KOREANA and the Kerasys brand are official partners of one of the largest K-POP events — the kpop-stars.kz festival.

We support this vibrant event every year. Over two days, the festival gathers a huge number of guests, participants, and fans of Korean culture. The atmosphere, organization, and audience engagement are always at the highest level.

For us, it's not just about participating in an event — it's about building brand awareness, strengthening emotional connections with a young audience, and elevating the image of Korean brands in Kazakhstan.

National Award 'Pride of the Industry'

Based on the results of 2022, 2023, and 2025 (NACE 46.90.9), KOREANA LLP received the national award 'Pride of the Industry'.

This status is awarded to companies that make a significant contribution to Kazakhstan's economy, demonstrating high reliability and steady growth. National-level recognition confirms our leading position in the non-specialized wholesale trade sector.

We are proud of this award, as it reflects the quality of our work, our team's professionalism, and the trust of our partners. This achievement motivates us to keep growing, expanding our assortment, and providing the best service in the Republic of Kazakhstan.

Supporting Student Activities and Youth

KOREANA LLP actively invests in the future by regularly partnering with youth and student events.

We support open house days at leading universities and sponsor student initiatives and festivals. It's important for us to stand with the younger generation, helping them unlock their potential and creating opportunities for growth and networking.

Our company sees students and youth as future leaders, professionals, and innovators. By sponsoring such events, we not only raise brand awareness among a younger audience but also contribute to the educational and cultural environment of Kazakhstan.

Quality Standards and Certification

All products presented by KOREANA are officially registered and certified in the Republic of Kazakhstan.

We work exclusively with manufacturers that meet international quality standards, ensuring product safety, efficacy, and stability.

KOREANA is the official and exclusive representative of several Korean brands in Kazakhstan, guaranteeing product authenticity and direct supplies from the manufacturer. We strictly adhere to all norms and requirements at every stage of the supply chain.
